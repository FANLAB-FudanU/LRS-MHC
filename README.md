This scripts file includes nine parts: (1) reads mapping, (2) coverage estimation, (3) HLA typing, (4) SNP calling, (5) variants annotation, (6) construction of LRS-based MHC reference panel, (7) imputation, (8) PheWAS and conditional analysis, and (9) fine-mapping.

Please cite: Zhou, C., Gong, T., Li, S., Jin, L., and Fan, S. Long-read sequencing reveals novel genetic polymorphisms in the major histocompatibility complex region and their impacts on the Han Chinese population. Sci China Life Sci, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11427-024-2742-y
